# MAGI 1.10.1

Your Melee game. Your corner.

This release brings the public downloads up to date with the current MAGI workspace. It includes the changes developed after the previous public release, 1.8.0.

## What's new

- **Windows Play Mode:** launch and host Slippi Netplay Dolphin beside configurable live stats, opponent/set tracking, Cornerman, Oracle, recent form, practice focus, and notes. Select, reorder, and resize panels, or start from Minimal, Live Coaching, and Practice presets.
- **Fullscreen:** preserve the 4:3 game image, keep panels visible in the side space, or reveal them at the edge. Use Ctrl+Shift+F11 to return to your layout.
- **Integrated Cornerman:** coaching, live events, errors, and optional voice are delivered inside MAGI and Play Mode. The old separate popup window is removed. Tracking uses the hosted Dolphin's replay-saving configuration; live stats do not require an AI provider.
- **Guided setup:** a resumable first-run wizard for identity, replay imports, and optional Netplay Dolphin, Playback Dolphin, and Melee game-image paths.
- **Character review:** large character overviews, explained core stats, move/technique breakdowns, expandable details, and bounded action clips for supported moments with available replay evidence.
- **Optional ChatGPT plan coaching:** browser sign-in and consent, available account models, account switching, refresh, disconnect, and usage management. Eligibility and limits apply. API-key and local-model options remain available.
- **Appearance:** Liquid, Telemetry, Tournament, and Light are shared by the main app and Play Mode. Older CRT and Amber preferences migrate to Liquid.
- **Technical accuracy:** the single Ken Combo stat requires the finishing sequence FAIR → FAIR → DAIR, with that final DAIR taking the stock. Historical count/highlight repair and coaching accuracy instructions are included. Other observed shine sequences are not labeled as verified waveshines without wavedash evidence.

## Downloads and requirements

- **Windows x64:** use `MAGI-Setup-1.10.1.exe` to install or update MAGI, or `MAGI-1.10.1.exe` for the portable app.
- **macOS:** choose the Intel (`x64`) or Apple Silicon (`arm64`) DMG from the attached assets.
- **Linux x64:** download `MAGI-1.10.1.AppImage` and make it executable before opening it.

Use the attached assets for the platforms available on this release. Native Play Mode hosting and embedded replay playback are Windows-only; macOS and Linux use external Slippi Dolphin playback. MAGI includes neither Slippi Dolphin nor a Melee game image. Configure your controller in Dolphin before playing. Hosted AI and cloud voice have their own availability, charges, and usage limits.

## Getting started and upgrading

1. Close your previous MAGI instance before opening the new build. Installing or downloading source alone does not replace an already running app.
2. Install or open the new package. Check that Settings shows **MAGI 1.10.1**. Play Mode's toolbar should say **Fullscreen**, and Cornerman should appear inside the panels rather than in a separate popup.
3. Fresh installations open the setup wizard. Existing installations retain local settings and data; reopen the guide from Settings if needed.
4. Configure Netplay Dolphin and your game image under Settings → Play Mode settings. Game Theater uses Playback Dolphin separately. Enable Cornerman and confirm replay saving/player identity before tracking a live session.
5. Add an AI provider in Settings → AI only when you want generated coaching. Parsing, statistics, browsing, notes, and game hosting work without AI.

The database upgrade repairs legacy Marth Ken Combo counts and invalid highlights and clears outdated Marth character coaching caches. It retains imported games. A backup of your local `.magi-melee` folder before upgrading is useful for recovery. This release does not claim a complete audit of every pre-existing Melee detector or live verification of every AI provider. AI suggestions can be wrong; timestamps and clips exist for supported moments, not every claim.
