# MAGI 1.16.0

Your Melee game. Your corner.

This release adds a Best Character panel to the Dashboard and removes the Performance Lab page. It follows 1.15.0.

## What's new

- **Best Character on the Dashboard.** Under Recent Form, the Dashboard shows the wireframe of your best character with their record and one sentence: what you do well with them and what to work on. A button opens that character's page.
- **How "best" is decided.** By results: the highest win rate after discounting small samples, among characters with at least 10 decided games. A 10-0 pocket pick does not outrank hundreds of games at 60%. Draws and unfinished games count toward neither side. Until one character has 10 decided games, the panel says so and names the closest.
- **How strengths and weaknesses are decided.** The panel compares that character with your other characters combined on five measures: neutral win rate, openings converted (two or more hits), damage per opening, recoveries that made it back, and edgeguards that took the stock. Each is a total across games (for example 743 of 10,031 edgeguards), never an average of per-game percentages, and both tiles show the counts behind them. A measure is named only when it differs by 5% or more. With games on only one character there is nothing to compare against, and the panel says that.
- **Performance Lab is removed.** Its scorecard repeated the Dashboard and Trends, and its suggestions and review queue came from the same per-game averages. The page and its sidebar and command-palette entries are gone; an old link to it opens the Dashboard. Play Mode's Recent form module and the notes on individual games are unchanged.
- **Dashboard layout.** The whole-record analysis card now matches the height of the column beside it and scrolls inside it, so neither column leaves a blank area.

## Downloads and requirements

- **Windows x64:** use `MAGI-Setup-1.16.0.exe` to install or update MAGI, or `MAGI-1.16.0.exe` for the portable app.
- **macOS and Linux:** use the attached macOS or Linux package when one is listed for this release.

Use the attached assets for the platforms available on this release. Native Play Mode hosting, the input display and embedded replay playback are Windows-only; macOS and Linux use external Slippi Dolphin playback. MAGI includes neither Slippi Dolphin nor a Melee game image. Configure your controller in Dolphin before playing. Hosted AI and cloud voice have their own availability, charges, and usage limits.

## Getting started and upgrading

1. Close your previous MAGI instance before opening the new build. Installing or downloading source alone does not replace an already running app.
2. Install or open the new package. Check that Settings shows **MAGI 1.16.0**.
3. Existing installations keep their local settings and data. A backup of your local `.magi-melee` folder beforehand is useful for recovery.
4. If you kept a training log in Performance Lab, your entries are still in the local database, but this version has no page that shows or adds to them.
5. Add an AI provider in Settings → AI only when you want generated coaching. Parsing, statistics, browsing, notes, and game hosting work without AI.
