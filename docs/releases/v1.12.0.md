# MAGI 1.12.0

Your Melee game. Your corner.

This release brings the public downloads up to date with the current MAGI workspace. It includes the 1.11.0 detector accuracy audit, which was not published as a download, and the 1.12.0 coaching changes. The previous public release was 1.10.1.

## What's new

- **Every AI analysis answers for the data you clicked.** A game review opened from the Library reports what happened in that game, with the count and the timestamp behind each finding; your history only decides whether to call something a habit. Character coaching covers only the games you played as that character, never games against it.
- **A read on your whole record, on the Dashboard.** MAGI now analyzes every game you have imported, looking for what a coach cannot hold in their head: a decision you keep making across characters and matchups and which matchups punish it, what changed between your oldest and newest games, and what separates your wins from your losses. It is generated once, kept with the number of games it covered, and can be refreshed when new games arrive.
- **Insight, not a stat recap.** Coaching findings must cite measured evidence with sample sizes, state the cost, and give a replacement option, under fixed headings (The Read, Fix This First, Also Worth Fixing, Exploit Next Game, Keep Doing, Practice Plan, and Only the Full Record Shows on the Dashboard). Every coaching request also sees your measured habits for the matchup and the character, with deviation flags and sample guards.
- **Detector accuracy audit (1.11.0).** Grab and throw move ids were shifted by one, so throw names in combo strings, kill moves and coaching were wrong and Ice Climbers wobbles could never be detected. Spike, shine-spike and offstage-kill evidence now reads the victim's position when the final hit landed. Battlefield and Dreamland offstage boundaries were swapped. Edgeguard success only counts after a hit or ledge occupation by the edgeguarder. Overclaiming highlights were renamed or tightened: "Early F-Smash Kill (tipper not verified)", Comeback requires a two-stock deficit, "Gerudo Dragon Kill", Sacred Combo is knee → Falcon Punch. Peach float cancels, Fox and Falco laser counts, and every special-move hit count now come from the replay states and records they claim to measure. Details and evidence: `docs/melee-accuracy.md`.
- **Measured DI.** Star KOs are no longer mislabeled as self-destructs; a death with no final hit but a live kill conversion is reported as a failed recovery.

## Downloads and requirements

- **Windows x64:** use `MAGI-Setup-1.12.0.exe` to install or update MAGI, or `MAGI-1.12.0.exe` for the portable app.
- **macOS (Apple Silicon):** `MAGI-1.12.0-arm64.dmg`. An Intel build is included only if an `x64` DMG appears in the attached assets.
- **Linux x64:** download `MAGI-1.12.0.AppImage` and make it executable before opening it.

Use the attached assets for the platforms available on this release. Native Play Mode hosting and embedded replay playback are Windows-only; macOS and Linux use external Slippi Dolphin playback. MAGI includes neither Slippi Dolphin nor a Melee game image. Configure your controller in Dolphin before playing. Hosted AI and cloud voice have their own availability, charges, and usage limits.

## Getting started and upgrading

1. Close your previous MAGI instance before opening the new build. Installing or downloading source alone does not replace an already running app.
2. Install or open the new package. Check that Settings shows **MAGI 1.12.0**.
3. Fresh installations open the setup wizard. Existing installations retain local settings and data; reopen the guide from Settings if needed.
4. Configure Netplay Dolphin and your game image under Settings → Play Mode settings. Game Theater uses Playback Dolphin separately.
5. Add an AI provider in Settings → AI only when you want generated coaching. Parsing, statistics, browsing, notes, and game hosting work without AI. The Dashboard's whole-record analysis needs an AI provider and at least 10 imported games.

The database upgrade relabels or removes highlights whose old detectors overclaimed and keeps every imported game. Games imported by an earlier version keep the per-game stats and event records that version produced; the coaching history blocks only trust death verdicts and punish rates from games parsed by the current detectors, and say so. Re-importing a replay folder does not reprocess games MAGI already has; a source checkout can rebuild everything with `npm run backfill`. Coaching cached by earlier versions is regenerated the next time you open it. A backup of your local `.magi-melee` folder before upgrading is useful for recovery. AI suggestions can be wrong; timestamps and clips exist for supported moments, not every claim.
